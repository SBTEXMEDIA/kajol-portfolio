Who we are

Every apparel trimming, under one roof.

"Customer first, quality always" isn't only a slogan — it's how Digitex Fashion has grown from a single unit in 2004 into a fully integrated trimmings manufacturer.

We run our own printing, label, elastic, twill-tape, poly and gum-tape units, so international buyers and factories get consistency, speed and control from a single supplier — instead of coordinating a dozen vendors. Every trim is produced to export standard and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 requirements.

Manufacturing units

Six units. One integrated factory.

Vertical integration means we control quality, lead time and cost from raw material to finished trim.

Label unit

Label Unit

Flexo & offset printing machines, label oven dryers and automatic cutting for size, care and barcode labels.

2Mlabels per day
Offset unit

Offset Printing Unit

Heidelberg (Germany) offset presses, auto punch, plate-making and lamination for hang tags, inlays, back board and stickers.

80Khang tags per day
Elastic unit

Elastic Unit

Crochet machines (Jiasheng, Cky), high-speed jacquard loom and Dahu / Credit Ocean warping for waistband and jacquard elastic.

20Kyards per day
Twill unit

Twill Tape Unit

Taiwan-built 6- and 8-needle looms with automatic warping, producing all types of woven twill tape.

20Kyards per day
Poly unit

Poly Unit

PP & LDPE blowing film, 6-colour flexo graphic printing, side/bottom sealing and recycling for PP & LDPE poly bags.

150Kbags per day
Gum tape

Gum Tape Unit

Paper-tube cutting, gum-tape winding and rewinding machinery for BOPP and gum tape rolls.

10Krolls per day

Compliance & certifications

Tested, traceable and export-ready
